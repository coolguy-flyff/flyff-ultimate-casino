House rules & odds assumptions
  • Every roll is uniform within its printed range — Gala doesn't publish the curves, so the felt assumes the fairest possible table.
  • Attack Speed, Critical Damage, Critical Chance, Block Penetration, Lifesteal, and Attack roll in 0.1 increments; every other stat rolls whole points.
  • Orange ranges are the yellow ranges halved and floored to the stat's increment: HP 4~9 becomes 2~4, and Lifesteal 0.8~1.8 becomes 0.4~0.9.
  • Ultimate stats never repeat on one weapon — not within a color, not across yellow and orange. Rerolls respect whatever the other color currently holds.
  • A Scroll of Ultimate Power rerolls every base line together; the Bonus scrolls reroll both lines of their color together.
  • Reaching +6 / +10 rolls the newly unlocked orange line on the house — this establishment only bills you for scrolls.
